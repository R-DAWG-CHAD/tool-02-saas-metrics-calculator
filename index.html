Understanding SaaS Unit Economics Benchmarks

Building a scalable SaaS business requires continuous monitoring of core operational metrics. Top venture capitalists evaluate early-stage software companies using standard benchmarks:

  • LTV:CAC Ratio > 3.0x: An LTV:CAC ratio above 3.0 indicates strong product-market fit and efficient customer acquisition. Ratios below 1.0 mean you are losing money on every customer.
  • CAC Payback Period < 12 Months: Capital-efficient SaaS startups aim to recover customer acquisition costs in less than 12 months. Payback periods exceeding 18-24 months indicate heavy cash burn risk.
  • SaaS Quick Ratio > 4.0: Calculated as (New MRR + Expansion MRR) / (Contraction MRR + Churned MRR). A Quick Ratio above 4.0 demonstrates hyper-growth efficiency.
